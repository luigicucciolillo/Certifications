# Deep-Tech Business Development

This repository documents my participation in business-development and venture-building programs within the **European Institute of Innovation and Technology (EIT) ecosystem**.

I joined these programs as **Co-Founder and Technical Lead of YesBAT**, with the goal of complementing the technical development of the project with structured business-development skills.

The repository contains program information and certificates of attendance.

## YesBAT

YesBAT is a deep-tech project focused on battery lifecycle management, using machine-learning-based (propetary) battery health analysis to support decisions around reuse, repair, second-life applications, and recycling.

## Timeline

**January 2026 — YesBAT started**
Initial technical development focused on battery health estimation, lifecycle decision support, system architecture, and solution feasibility.

**May–July 2026 — BGI · EIT Urban Mobility**
Venture-building activities focused on problem–solution fit, customer segments, value proposition, business-model design, MVP definition, and go-to-market strategy.

**July–September 2026 — EIT Jumpstarter**
Focused on market segmentation, customer value proposition, competitive advantage, financial planning, business-plan development, and pitching.

**July–November 2026 — 28DIGITAL · Mentor Me**
Mentoring activities supported product–market fit, business strategy, sales, funding readiness, and investor communication.
